Friend Time

November 20, 2026

I once told my friend, “You can be obese with friendship. That is why I am on a friendship diet.”

I think that my maximum for extremely gooning friend time (no productivity, no coding, just gooning all the time) in any given day is around one hour.

I am not an introvert. But I think that once you start hanging out with people too much, it gets weird. You start being really darn close friends, and sometimes, they are girls. I believe that scientifically, if a straight girl and a straight guy are really close friends, then at least one of them likes the other. It must be a fact. There is simply no way there is no way.
