Why I Write

November 20, 2026

I think that I should start writing again, because everything is so abundant now. I can generate 10 essays in 1 minute, and I can code a website in 10 minutes with some vague prompt.

I keep on context switching, and writing prevents me from doing that. I feel incredibly sad and disappointed whenever I keep on context switching. It is like a poison.

I am slowly going to become a bit more smart and a bit less dumb in the head. As AI gets smarter, I need to become smarter. Imagine all these dumb people that have lost the ability to think because of AI. Boom, I will go in with my turbo brain and crush them, but that means I need to have a turbo brain.

Stop context switching. Stop switching windows. Think and be calm with yourself.

Writing is my way to stop being such a goon all the time.
