Rant

November 20, 2026

I have been using AI a lot, and it has made me sloppy. I have been on my phone a lot, and it has made me distracted. I am more lazy than I used to be. I cannot remember things clearly, I have many more reminders. I used to tell Claude beautiful, well thought out prompts that were well thought out. Now, I tell Claude “yeah go do it make it work” and I get away with it. I crave social media more, even though I used to take pride in not engaging in social media. I lack the drive that I used to. I want to make money and be happy and successful, I want to marry a pretty, kind girl that I can care for, I want to have kids. I want to have a daughter, in particular. I want to be rich because I want to care for them, I want to be incredibly successful and AI savvy and AI great and AI awesome, but also a genius and focused and hold clarity in scary large amounts. I tell myself I will read books, I will sit in silence and journal with real pen and paper. I tell myself I will dopamine detox, but it does not seem to come to fruition. I want to succeed so badly, but I seem to not understand what it takes. I wonder if success is something that I really desire, or if I just like the feeling of desiring it.
